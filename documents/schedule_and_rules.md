## Event Overview

**TechStorm 2026** is a 2-day campus tech extravaganza featuring competitive esports, a 24-hour hackathon, cosplay, sponsor activations, and more. Hosted at the iconic Nexus Convention Center in Koramangala, the fest brings together 2000+ participants from colleges across Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh, and Telangana.

### Key Numbers
- **4 Esports Tournaments**: Valorant, BGMI, FIFA 25, Rocket League
- **1 Hackathon**: 24-hour continuous build (starts Day 1 evening)
- **1 Cosplay Competition**: Open registration, judged on Day 2
- **5 Title & Gold Sponsors**: HyperX, Monster Energy, Logitech, AMD, Jio
- **Total Prize Pool**: ₹3,00,000+

### Venue Hours
- **Day 1**: Gates open 8:00 AM | Events 9:00 AM – 11:00 PM
- **Day 2**: Gates open 8:00 AM | Events 9:00 AM – 9:00 PM (Awards Ceremony at 7:00 PM)

## General Rules

### Participant Conduct
1. **Valid ID required**: College ID + Government photo ID for age verification
2. **Age requirement**: 16+ for general entry, 18+ for esports registration
3. **No substance abuse**: Alcohol and drugs strictly prohibited. Violators will be removed immediately.
4. **Respectful behavior**: Zero tolerance for harassment, hate speech, or discrimination. Report incidents to any volunteer (orange T-shirts).
5. **Property care**: Damage to equipment will be charged to the responsible participant/team.

### Tournament Conduct
1. **Be on time**: Teams must check in 15 minutes before their scheduled match. No-shows after 10 minutes = forfeit (walkover).
2. **No coaching during matches**: Coaches/managers must stay behind the designated line.
3. **Stream sniping**: Strictly prohibited. Participants must not have any live streams of their own match visible.
4. **Communication**: In-game comms only via team voice chat. No external calls during live matches.
5. **Hardware**: PC tournaments use venue hardware. Participants may bring their own peripherals (mouse, keyboard, headset) for PC tournaments.
6. **Disconnection policy**:
   - If within first 3 rounds (Valorant) or first 20 minutes (BGMI/FIFA): pause and reconnect within 5 minutes, or rematch at admin discretion.
   - If after that point: match continues, disconnected team plays with fewer players.

### Venue Rules
1. **Wristband must be visible** at all times. Lost wristband = ₹200 replacement fee.
2. **No outside food** in Arena Alpha or Arena Beta. Food Court and designated eating areas only.
3. **Photography**: Personal photography allowed. Flash photography during matches prohibited.
4. **Lost & Found**: At the Registration desk.
5. **Smoking**: Designated area behind the Food Court only.

## Dispute Resolution Process

### Step 1: Report
- Go to the **#public-helpdesk** channel (or visit the Registration desk in person)
- Provide: Match ID, team names, the issue, and any evidence (screenshots, video clips)
- The concierge agent will format a **Dispute Report** and escalate to tournament ops

### Step 2: Investigation
- The bracket marshal reviews evidence within **15 minutes** of receiving the report
- Both teams may be asked to provide their perspective
- Admin spectator recordings (if available) will be reviewed

### Step 3: Resolution
- The bracket marshal drafts a recommendation (rematch, uphold result, or disqualification)
- The **Fest Lead** must approve the resolution (no automated decisions)
- Resolution is communicated to both teams and announced in #public-helpdesk

### Step 4: Appeal
- Teams may appeal **once** within 10 minutes of the resolution announcement
- Appeals are reviewed by a **3-person panel** (Fest Lead + 2 senior volunteers)
- Panel decision is **final and binding**

### Dispute Types & Typical Outcomes
| Issue | Typical Resolution |
|-------|-------------------|
| Alleged cheating (aim assist, macros) | Review admin spectator POV → DQ if confirmed |
| Disconnection dispute | Apply disconnection policy (see General Rules) |
| Wrong score reported | Cross-reference with game server logs |
| Player substitution violation | Forfeit the map where violation occurred |
| Unsportsmanlike conduct | Warning → Match forfeit → Tournament DQ (3-strike) |
